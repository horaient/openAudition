The standard.

The HORAI Code, version 1. Read it before you apply. We judge what you can show, not what you say.

  • Presence on stageAttention that arrives before you do.
  • Mindset, voice and bearingHow you speak, walk and behave, on and off stage.
  • Passion and obsessionPlus smart, hard work. Talent alone does not coast here.
  • Action and seriousnessRespect for the stage, the rules and the team.
  • Big visionDream big, then act on it.
  • OriginalityYour own voice and movement. No clones, no imitation.

Lines never crossed

Violation of humanity, intolerance, bias, bullying, racism, excessive ego, and damaging your reputation without handling it wisely.
